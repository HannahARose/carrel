# The v0.4 gate

v0.4 ships when the owner can find any filed item by browsing or searching (issue #5, `docs/design/search.md`). This is the checklist for that run, and the record of it. Like v0.3's, it's run with the other gates before 1.0 (#38).

It tests finding things in your own words: twenty items you remember, described the way you'd describe them, not by their titles. The findability benchmark (`carrel bench --search`) measures the same thing on the test set; this is the check on your real archive.

## Before

- [ ] **Carrel:** build and install `dev` (`docs/guide/getting-started.md`), so `carrel --version` is the latest.
- [ ] **The embedding model:** `carrel models download embeddinggemma-300m` (334 MB), or `--format mlx` for the MLX build (361 MB).
- [ ] **The index:** `carrel index rebuild`, then `carrel index status` says every item is embedded and none are behind.
- [ ] **Your twenty items:** before searching, write down twenty things you know are in the archive, each as a sentence in your own words ("the lease for the flat on Cowley Road", "that paper about light-sheet microscopes"). Mix them up:
  - documents with their own text (PDFs, letters, statements);
  - images, scans and folders;
  - something filed long ago and something filed this week;
  - two or three found by an exact name or number ("invoice 2057-4038");
  - one or two restricted or sensitive items.

## The run

1. [ ] **Search for each one** with `carrel search "…"`. Write down where it came in the results (1st, 3rd, not in the top ten), and whether the reason shown (the passage, the words highlighted) made sense.
2. [ ] **The same in Browse:** open `carrel`, press `/` in Browse, and search for five of them. The results should match `carrel search`'s.
3. [ ] **Filters:** narrow three searches with a filter (`collection:`, `tag:`, `kind:`, `after:`, `before:`, `sensitivity:`, `checked-out`), and check the item stays and the noise goes.
4. [ ] **Around an item:** for five of the items you found, open them in Browse and check:
   - **History** lists every change that touched it, from filing on;
   - **Related** shows its links and the items nearest it in meaning, and they're actually related;
   - Enter on a related item goes to it.
5. [ ] **Restricted items:** search for a phrase you know is in a restricted item's text, such as a word from a bank statement. It should come back by its card, if at all, and never with that passage shown.
6. [ ] **Recent:** the Status screen's Recent shows what you filed, changed or worked on lately.
7. [ ] **Keeping current:** correct or edit one item's card so it says something new, wait a moment, and search for the new words by meaning. `carrel index status` should show it caught up.
8. [ ] **Switch models (optional):** `carrel index model qwen3-embedding-0.6b` (after downloading it, 639 MB), and search while it builds: results keep coming from the old index. Run the twenty searches again on the new model, and switch back if it's no better.
9. [ ] **From your editor (optional):** `:CarrelSearch` in Neovim or `carrel-search` in Emacs (`docs/guide`) finds the same items.

## Passing

The gate passes when:

- at least eighteen of the twenty come back in the top five, and all twenty in the top ten;
- exact names and numbers come back first;
- no restricted item's text is ever shown or matched;
- and the owner is willing to use search instead of remembering where things were filed.

Anything that doesn't pass becomes an issue in the v0.4 milestone, or a later one if it can wait, with the searches that failed.
