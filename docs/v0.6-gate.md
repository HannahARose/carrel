# The v0.6 gate

v0.6 ships when the owner spends an hour with the librarian and the discovery views, finds something they'd forgotten, learns something about the archive they didn't know, and answers questions that improve at least ten cards (issue #6, `docs/design/librarian.md`). This is the checklist for that run, and the record of it. Like the gates before it, it's run with the others before 1.0 (#38).

It tests the archive as something to talk to and wander through, on your real archive, in every interface: the command line, the interactive mode and the Mac app. The librarian and question-value benchmarks (`carrel bench --librarian`, `carrel bench --questions`, `docs/bench/README.md`) measure the same things on the test set; this is the check on what you actually keep. How each part works is in the [user guide](guide/user-guide.md#asking-the-librarian) and [the Mac app's guide](guide/mac-app.md#the-librarian).

## Before

- [ ] **Carrel:** build and install `dev` (`docs/guide/getting-started.md`), and the Mac app from the same commit (the **Mac app** job's `Carrel.app` artifact, or Xcode), so `carrel --version` and the app agree.
- [ ] **The Mac app's questions:** suggested answers, skipping and the new kinds on its Questions screen (#319), and questions in the inspector, on a wander, from the librarian and on Status (#316), are built, since steps 5 and 7 use them.
- [ ] **The index:** `carrel index status` says every item is embedded and none are behind; the librarian searches through it.
- [ ] **The librarian's model:** `carrel settings` shows which one it answers with. The larger the better: on a 32 GB Mac, `carrel models download qwen3.5-9b` (5.7 GB, or `--format mlx` for the MLX build, 6 GB) if it isn't installed. Write down which model each interface says it used.
- [ ] **A few things you remember,** written down before you start, to ask about: a subject you have several items on, a document whose contents you'd like summarized, and a stretch of time ("what did I work on last spring?"). Also a restricted item you know is there, such as a bank statement, and a phrase from its text.
- [ ] **`librarian.changes` is `off`** (`carrel settings`), as it is unless you've turned it on. Step 6 turns it on, if you choose to.
- [ ] **An hour,** with the archive to yourself: nothing filing in the background that you'd rather review first.

## The run

Spend about twenty minutes in each interface. The steps can come in any order; what matters is that each is tried somewhere, and the librarian, questions and discovery in all three.

1. [ ] **Ask the librarian at the command line:**
   - `carrel ask "…"` for one of your subjects. The answer names the items, quotes the passages it read, and ends with the model and its steps.
   - `carrel ask` for a conversation: three or four questions, each building on the last ("and what about the year before?"). Answer any question it asks about an item, or leave it with an empty line.
   - Ask for the document's summary, and check the passages are really in it.
   - Ask about the restricted item: it may say it exists, by its card, but it never quotes its text, even when you ask for the phrase.
   - Save one conversation with `--save`, and open this month's Journal in an editor: the questions, answers and links read well without Carrel.
2. [ ] **The Librarian screen** (`L` in the interactive mode): ask about your stretch of time. Choose items with `↑↓` and check the card on the right, open one in Browse with `o`, answer its question with `a` if it asks one, save with `s` and start again with `n`.
3. [ ] **The Librarian panel** in the Mac app (⌥⌘L): ask a question of your own, follow an item into Browse, then choose another item and **Ask About This** (⇧⌘L). **Save to Journal**, and check the Journal has both the terminal's and the app's conversations.
4. [ ] **Suggestions, with `librarian.changes` off:** when the librarian suggests a change (a refile, a corrected title, a rename, a new collection), make one from each interface: the command it prints at the command line, `y` on the Librarian screen, **Review This Change…** in the panel. Each is shown before it's saved, and History lists it as your own change. Decline one, and check nothing changed.
5. [ ] **Questions in the queue:** in `carrel questions`, the Questions screen (`4`) and the app's Questions:
   - the most useful are first, and each shows the items it's about and suggested answers;
   - answer some by picking a suggestion, some in your own words, across the kinds: a term, a collection or group, a sender or project (say whether to add a routing rule), and an item;
   - an item's answer is shown as a correction before and after, and saved only on your yes; a lasting fact offers to join the glossary;
   - skip one (`s`, `carrel questions skip ID`) and check it's gone from the list but in `carrel questions --all`, resting;
   - dismiss one.
6. [ ] **The librarian's own changes (optional):** `carrel settings set librarian.changes on` (or in the interactive mode's or the app's Settings), and leave `librarian.confidence` at 0.9. Ask a few questions likely to turn up a misfiled item or a vague title. Then:
   - each change it made says so under its answer, and History marks it **by the librarian**;
   - none touched a field you wrote yourself or a restricted item, and none deleted anything, edited a file, changed a setting or a rule;
   - `carrel status`, the interactive mode's Status and the Librarian screen, and the app's Status and panel list **the librarian's changes this week**;
   - undo one from each interface: `carrel undo CHANGE`, `u` on the Librarian screen, **Undo** in the panel;
   - undo the rest together: `carrel undo --librarian today`, `U` on the Librarian screen, or **Undo All…** in the app, and check `carrel log --librarian` shows each undone and the cards are as they were;
   - turn it off again, or leave it on if you'd like to keep it.
7. [ ] **Questions as you go:**
   - in Browse, open items until one shows **Carrel asks** above its card and answer it with `a`; `carrel show` lists the same question; in the app, answer one in the inspector;
   - on a wander, answer a question a step asks (`a` in Discover; the printed command after `carrel discover wander`; in the app's Wander);
   - answer one the librarian asks after an answer, in each interface;
   - `carrel status` (and the interactive mode's and the app's Status) names a few questions today; the same ones all day, and a skipped one doesn't come back on Status.
8. [ ] **On this day:** `carrel discover`, `d` in Discover (`D`), and **On This Day** in the app agree. Look back from another date (`--date`, the app's date picker), such as a birthday or the start of a project.
9. [ ] **Wander the stacks:** take at least twenty steps in each interface: `carrel discover wander` (`Enter` for the next), `w` in Discover (`Space`, with previews where the terminal draws them), and **Wander** in the app (→, with previews). Each step says why it came next. Note anything you'd forgotten you had. Start a walk from an item (`carrel discover wander ITEM`), and check `--seed N` takes the same steps again.
10. [ ] **The timeline:** `carrel discover timeline` by month, then `--per year` and `--per day`; `--by worked` to see what you worked on when; with a filter such as `tag:receipts after:2024`. The same in Discover (`t`, `p`) and the app's **Timeline** (**By** and **Per**). Look for a gap or a busy stretch you didn't expect.
11. [ ] **The archive map:** `carrel discover map`, `m` in Discover, and the app's **Archive Map** by size and by items, zooming into the largest collection. Does what takes up the space surprise you?
12. [ ] **Activity:** `carrel discover activity`, `h` in Discover, and the app's **Activity**. The busy days match what you remember doing.
13. [ ] **Resurfacing:** if Status names a collection not opened in a while, in any interface, open it from there; it's not named again for a week. If none is named during the hour, note that.
14. [ ] **Every interface agrees:** the librarian's answers to the same question in each interface find the same items (the wording may differ); the discovery views show the same items and counts; History shows every answer and change, from whichever interface, with the device it was made on.

## Passing

The gate passes when:

- within the hour, the owner finds something they'd forgotten and learns something about the archive they didn't know;
- answers to questions improve at least ten cards, each change shown before it was saved and listed in History;
- the librarian's answers name the right items, its passages are really in them, and it never quotes a restricted item's text;
- it never changed anything with `librarian.changes` off, and with it on, it made only the kinds of change it may, each marked as its own and undone, one at a time and all together, from every interface;
- a saved conversation is in the Journal, readable without Carrel, and nothing unsaved was written into the archive;
- every step above worked in the command line, the interactive mode and the Mac app, and they never disagreed about the archive;
- and the owner would come back to the librarian and the discovery views, rather than only searching.

Anything that doesn't pass becomes an issue in the v0.6 milestone, fixed before the release, or moved to a later milestone if it can wait, with the questions asked and the answers given.
