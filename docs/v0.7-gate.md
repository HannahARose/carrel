# The v0.7 gate

v0.7 ships when the first whole-archive restructure is reviewed and approved, its findability test shows the new shape is easier to navigate, and undoing it puts everything back (issue #34, `docs/design/restructure.md`). This is the checklist for that run, and the record of it. Like the gates before it, it's run with the others before 1.0 (#38).

It tests reorganizing a real archive, in every interface: the command line, the interactive mode and the Mac app. The restructure and crawler benchmarks (`carrel bench --crawl`, `docs/bench/README.md`) measure the same things on a deliberately muddled copy of the test set; this is the check on the archive you actually keep. How each part works is in the [user guide](guide/user-guide.md#reorganizing-the-archive) and [the Mac app's guide](guide/mac-app.md#restructure).

## Before

- [ ] **Carrel:** build and install `dev` (`docs/guide/getting-started.md`), and the Mac app from the same commit (the **Mac app** job's `Carrel.app` artifact, or Xcode), so `carrel --version` and the app agree.
- [ ] **The Mac app's crawler, eagerness and section proposals** (#369) are built, since steps 4, 6 and 8 use them.
- [ ] **A backup:** `carrel backup list` shows backups are set up and the drive is connected, and `carrel backup now` takes a snapshot. Approving a restructure takes another, but this one is from before you started.
- [ ] **The index:** `carrel index status` says every item is embedded and none are behind. Grouping uses it, and without it a proposal is weaker.
- [ ] **The model:** `carrel settings` shows the librarian's model, which also names groups and runs the findability test. The larger the better: on a 32 GB Mac, `carrel models download qwen3.5-9b` (5.7 GB) if it isn't installed. The catalog has no 20–30B model yet; note which model each step used.
- [ ] **A record of the archive as it is,** to compare after undoing (step 9). In the archive's folder:
  - `find . -path ./_System -prune -o -type f -print | sort > ~/carrel-before-files.txt`, every file and where it is;
  - `carrel discover map --json > ~/carrel-before-map.json`, the collections and their counts;
  - `carrel restructure eagerness` and `carrel settings`, written down.
- [ ] **No checkouts you mind leaving out:** `carrel checkouts`. A restructure never moves a checked-out item or a collection holding one, so return what you can first.
- [ ] **`crawler.changes` is `off`** (`carrel settings`), as it is unless you've turned it on. Step 7 turns it on, if you choose to.
- [ ] **An hour or two,** with the archive to yourself: nothing filing in the background that you'd rather review first.

## The run

The steps can come in any order, except that the whole-archive restructure (step 5) is approved before it's undone (step 9). What matters is that each part is tried somewhere, and proposals, eagerness and the crawler in all three interfaces.

1. [ ] **The signs:** `carrel status`, the interactive mode's Status and the app's Status show the same **Structure** signals: crowded or single-item collections, a tag across many collections, a lot in Holding, items in different branches often linked or worked on together, siblings divided very differently. Note which ones you agree with.
2. [ ] **The graph:** `carrel discover graph`, then `--items`; `g` in Discover (`c` between collections and items); **Connections** in the app's Discover. They show the same strongest connections, and each says why (shared tags, links, days worked on together). Note any tie you didn't know about.
3. [ ] **Tags, at the command line:** `carrel restructure tags`, then `carrel restructure tags show`:
   - each merge, split and retirement says why and which items it changes;
   - tags you wrote yourself, and checked-out items' tags, are listed as left alone;
   - approve part of it with `--only`, and check the cards, `ARCHIVE.md` and the collections' vocabularies changed as shown, in one change in `carrel log`;
   - `carrel undo` that change, and check the tags are as they were.
4. [ ] **Eagerness, in each interface:**
   - `carrel restructure eagerness` shows the archive's level and the numbers behind it;
   - set a collection you know by heart to `settled`: `carrel restructure eagerness COLLECTION settled`, `E` on it in Browse, or **Eagerness** in its inspector in the app; check the other two show it, and that a collection inside it says it's set by its parent;
   - set a messy collection to `eager`, from another interface;
   - each is a change in History; undo one, and set it again.
5. [ ] **The first whole-archive restructure:** `carrel restructure propose` (no collection), then `carrel restructure show`. Read it as you would anyone's proposal:
   - the rationale, cost, benefit and **findability result**: how often held-out cards were found in the proposed tree and in the current one. It goes forward only if the new tree does better; if it says it's untested, the model didn't run, and the gate isn't passed until it has;
   - the changes, grouped by how invasive they are, from rewriting design documents to moving top-level collections, each with why;
   - what it held back, and why: sealed bundles moved whole, checked-out items, the settled collection from step 4 asking more of a change;
   - nothing it proposes deletes anything or changes a field you wrote.

   Then open it in the interactive mode (`f` on Review) and the app's **Restructure**: the same changes, before and after side by side. Leave out the changes you don't want (`Space`, or untick them in the app), check that a change needing one you left out is dropped too and said so, and **approve the rest** in whichever interface you like. Note how many you approved, by kind.
6. [ ] **After approving:**
   - `carrel log` (and each interface's History) shows **one change**, with a backup snapshot before it;
   - the moved items are where the proposal said, their cards' links still lead to them, and the design documents name the new collections;
   - `carrel status` shows fewer of the signals the proposal set out to resolve;
   - a checked-out item, if there was one, is where you left it.
7. [ ] **Use the new shape,** for a while, from each interface:
   - find five items you know are there by browsing alone, without search, in the new tree. Note how many you found, and how many steps each took;
   - search and the librarian still find what they did;
   - propose a smaller restructure for one collection: `carrel restructure propose COLLECTION`, `R` on it in Browse, **Propose Changes Here** in the app. Approve it, or reject it and check nothing changed.
8. [ ] **The crawler:**
   - `carrel crawl status`, the crawler's findings in the interactive mode (`f` on Review) and **The Crawler** on the app's Status agree on how far it has got and what's due;
   - `carrel crawl run` (or `r`, or **Check Some Now**) checks a few items now; its findings, moves and corrections, wait in Review, each with why. Approve one and reject one in each interface. **No field you wrote yourself is corrected;**
   - optionally, `carrel settings set crawler.changes on` and let it run (in `carrel agent`, or the app's agent when the Mac is idle and on power). Its changes are marked **by the crawler** in History and listed on Status; undo one, then the rest together (`carrel crawl undo 7d`, `u` in the interactive mode, **Undo All of Them…** in the app). Turn it off again, or leave it on;
   - it never proposed or made a whole-section restructure on its own.
9. [ ] **Undo the whole-archive restructure:** `carrel undo CHANGE` with the change from step 5 (or `u` on it in History, or **Undo Change…** in the app). Undo step 7's restructure first if you approved it, or note what the undo says about it. Then, in the archive's folder:
   - `find . -path ./_System -prune -o -type f -print | sort | diff ~/carrel-before-files.txt -` shows only what you added or changed yourself since;
   - `carrel discover map --json | diff ~/carrel-before-map.json -` shows the same collections and counts, apart from the same;
   - the design documents, tags and links are as they were, and `carrel status` shows the signals from step 1 again.

   Then **approve it again**, if you'd like to keep the new shape: propose and approve, or keep the old one.
10. [ ] **Every interface agrees:** the proposals, their changes and their findability results are the same in each interface; History shows every approval, undo and eagerness change, from whichever interface, with the device it was made on.

## Passing

The gate passes when:

- the first whole-archive restructure was proposed by a model, reviewed change by change, and approved in whole or in part;
- its findability test found held-out cards more often in the new shape than in the old, and finding things by browsing in the new shape felt at least as easy;
- approving it was one change with one snapshot, and undoing it put every item, folder, design document, tag and link back as they were;
- nothing was deleted, no field you wrote was changed, no sealed bundle was entered and no checked-out item was moved;
- tag proposals, eagerness and the crawler did what the guide says, and nothing was reorganized without your approval;
- every step above worked in the command line, the interactive mode and the Mac app, and they never disagreed about the archive;
- and the owner would let Carrel propose the next restructure.

Anything that doesn't pass becomes an issue in the v0.7 milestone, fixed before the release, or moved to a later milestone if it can wait.
