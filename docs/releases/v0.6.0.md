Carrel's librarian is finished. Ask it about what's in your archive, in your own words, and it answers with the items and passages it used. Its suggestions can be made in a click, and each one is undoable. Discovery shows what happened on this day in other years, wanders the stacks, and draws a timeline, a map and your activity. Questions now come at natural moments: while you browse, on a wander, after an answer, and a few a day on Status. Everything Carrel can do is in the command line, the interactive mode and the Mac app, down to each setting. Everything still stays on your computer. This is a 0.x release, so anything may change before 1.0.

New here? Start with [Getting started](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/Getting-Started), and for the librarian, [Asking the librarian](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/User-Guide#asking-the-librarian) in the user guide.

## The librarian

- **Ask anything** with `carrel ask`, on the interactive mode's Librarian screen (`L`), or in the Mac app's Librarian panel (⌥⌘L), with "Ask About This" on any item (⇧⌘L).
- **What it shows:** each answer lists the items and passages it used, and the model that gave it. A conversation can be saved to this month's Journal.
- **What it suggests:** refiling an item, correcting a card's field, renaming an item, or creating a collection. Each suggestion is made in a click.
- **Changes on its own:** with `librarian.changes` on, it makes the changes it's at least `librarian.confidence` sure of itself. Each one is undoable, listed on Status and in History, and they can be undone together.
- **More reliable answers:**
  - it no longer runs out of room partway through an answer;
  - it doesn't repeat a tool call it has already made;
  - it trims what it reads to fit the model.

  On the benchmark, Qwen3.5 4B now finds 70% of the right items and states 89% of the facts. No model meets the recommended bar yet: Carrel uses the largest model you have installed that fits, then Apple's.
- **A missing model** is no longer a dead end. Every interface offers to download the librarian's model, and the embedding model search uses, with its size, a confirmation and progress, instead of pointing you to the command line.

## Discovery

- **On this day:** what was made, filed or worked on on this day in other years.
- **Wander the stacks:** one item at a time, each with why it came next. A walk can start from any item.
- **Timeline:** items by the date they were made, filed or worked on, narrowed by words and filters.
- **The archive map and your activity:** collections by size, and a heatmap of filing and work by day, over any range.

All of them are in `carrel discover`, the interactive mode's Discover screen (`D`) and the Mac app's sidebar.

## Questions

- **Every kind,** in every interface:
  - about a term, answered into the glossary;
  - about an item, as a card correction;
  - about a group, which becomes a collection;
  - about a sender, with an optional rule for where their future items go.
- **Ranked** by what an answer would settle, with suggested answers. A skipped question rests for `questions.skip_days`.
- **At natural moments:** a question about the item you're looking at, one now and then on a wander or after the librarian's answer, and a few a day on Status.

## Every interface fully capable

A full comparison of the three interfaces found the gaps, and v0.6 closes them.

- **Settings:** every setting can be changed from `carrel settings set`, the interactive mode's Settings and the Mac app's Settings. That includes three that had no setting before: where checkouts go, what happens to a returned working copy, and how long a pause ends a work session. A fourth, for reminders, is there for when they're built.
- **The Mac app gained:**
  - the intake queue, with Remove, Move to Front and Try Again;
  - entry by entry or whole when adding a folder;
  - the desk's title, summary, archiving and removal with its card;
  - returning a moved working copy;
  - Status's problems, names that won't work everywhere and desk suggestions;
  - related items;
  - editing a card's notes and body;
  - History's librarian filter;
  - Activity's date range, and Wander from Here;
  - an embedding model choice for a new archive;
  - Verify's amount, and where Restore puts an item.
- **The interactive mode gained:**
  - backup options: verify amount, restore folder and repository name;
  - the desk's title and summary, and returning a moved working copy;
  - the timeline's words and dates, Activity's range, and wandering from an item;
  - an embedding model for a new archive, and starting history for an archive without one;
  - asking for Carrel's questions about an item;
  - the librarian's model and image previews in Settings;
  - deleting a model.
- **Card bodies** can be edited everywhere: `carrel edit --body`, `E` in Browse, and the app's editor. Carrel's own sections stay as they are, and a body changed elsewhere meanwhile is never overwritten.
- **Returning a working copy** follows `checkout.after_return`: it goes to the Trash, is kept, or you're asked.

## Fixes

- **Switching tabs** in the interactive mode, and screens in the Mac app, no longer freezes for up to half a minute. Providers are found once a session, never while you wait.
- **The Mac app's Review and Questions screens** can be chosen again.
- **A review of everything merged for this release** found 20 bugs, and all of them are fixed. Among them:
  - a restructure interrupted partway could leave an item's contents and its card apart, with nothing for undo to restore;
  - an answer cut off on its last step lost what the librarian had found;
  - remembered provider errors were repeated.

## Ahead of v0.7

These are in this release, but they belong to v0.7, restructure proposals, and may change before it:

- **Restructure proposals:**
  - `carrel restructure` proposes a better shape for a section of the archive, or for your tags, as a list of changes with reasons;
  - you approve all, part or none of it, and it runs as one change, undone as a unit;
  - `restructure.eagerness` (settled, balanced or eager, archive-wide or per collection) says how readily Carrel proposes, scaled by how invasive each change is.
- **Crawlers:** in the background, when the device is idle and on power, they recheck where items are filed and how good their cards are. They suggest moves and corrections in Review; with `crawler.changes` on, they make the ones they're sure of, each undoable.
- **The tag and link graph:** `carrel discover graph`, a text view in the interactive mode, and a drawn graph in the Mac app.
- **`carrel report`:** a local, content-free log of Carrel's decisions, summarized to show what needs tweaking.

## Install

Download the archive for your platform below, unpack it, and put its programs together in a folder on your `PATH`. Each archive includes Kopia and every provider for its platform except `carrel-provider-cad`, with `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`.

- **Checksums** are in `SHA256SUMS`.
- **Build attestation:** each archive has one, from this repository's new home (`gh attestation verify FILE --repo Horizon-Labs/carrel`). Carrel moved to the Horizon-Labs organization in this release, and the old addresses redirect.
- **The Mac app:** signed builds come with v1.0. Until then, build it with Xcode, as [The Mac app](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/Mac-App) explains.

The [user guide](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/User-Guide) explains each part, and [Command-Line Tool](https://github.com/Horizon-Labs/carrel/wiki/Command-Line-Tool) lists every command.
