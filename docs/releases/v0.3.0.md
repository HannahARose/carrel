Carrel can now look after what you're working on, not just what you've filed. Check a project out of the archive, work on it with your usual tools, and Carrel notices the work as it happens and brings the new version back safely. Cards can be corrected or edited at any time, and Carrel reads more kinds of files: what's inside zips, 3D models and CAD files, and images with what they show. Everything still stays on your computer. This is a 0.x release, so anything may change before 1.0; the Mac app follows in v0.5.

New here? Start with [Getting started](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Getting-Started).

## Working on items

- **Checkout and return:** `carrel checkout ITEM` copies an item out to work on (to the Desktop, or `--to FOLDER`, with an optional `--due` date), makes the archive copy read-only, and on a Mac tags the copy **Checked Out** in Finder. `carrel return ITEM` shows what changed, the Git commits since, and the history entry it adds, then swaps the new version in safely after a snapshot. `--discard` cancels a checkout instead.
- **Work sessions, noticed for you:** Carrel looks for finished stretches of work on checked-out and desk items whenever it runs (at most every five minutes, and in the background while the interactive mode is open), from file changes and Git commits, and adds a pending history entry for each. With backups set up, each session takes a snapshot of the working copy, and returning offers to forget them once the returned version is safe. `snapshots: return-only` on a card skips them for large data.
- **History entries** can be approved, edited, combined or dropped, in `carrel sessions` or on the Active screen. Approving one by hand in Obsidian works too: Carrel notices and records it.
- **The desk:** `carrel desk add PATH` tracks a folder you're working on where it is, with a card beside it drafted by your text model. `Active/On desk/` mirrors each desk card in the archive, and notes you write there are copied back. `carrel desk archive PATH` files it into the archive with its card and history when you're done, and `carrel status` suggests folders to track and desk items to archive.
- **The Active screen** (`3` in the interactive mode) shows what's checked out and on the desk, with their pending entries: return (`r`), cancel (`x`), open the working copy (`o`), and approve, combine or drop entries.

## Fixing cards

- **Corrections in plain words:** `c` in Review or Browse, or `carrel correct ITEM "…"`. "It's from 2019" changes the date; "it's a picture of Annecy, not Geneva" rewrites the card and files it again where it fits. You see each change before it's saved, fields you state stay yours, and lines like `created: 2019-03-31` work without a model.
- **`carrel edit ITEM`** (`e` in Browse) opens a filed card's fields in your editor, checked as proposals are. Deleting a line clears it.
- **An item's history** in Browse and `carrel show`: every change that touched it, with undo from there. **Recent** on the Status screen shows the latest changes and work sessions, archive-wide.

## Reading more kinds of files

- **Compressed files are expanded,** so what's inside can be browsed: zip, tar and the common compressors, and 7z and RAR when 7-Zip or `unar` is installed. Drafting reads inside before anything is expanded, and expanding happens only when you approve, with checks against unsafe archives. Installers, encrypted or split archives, and very large expansions stay compressed or ask first; `compressed_files: keep` (or a routing rule) keeps them packed. The original goes to the Trash once the expanded copy checks out, and `carrel expand ITEM` expands one already filed.
- **3D models and CAD:** on a Mac, STL, OBJ, PLY and USDZ files are drawn from four angles and described by Apple's on-device model. 3MF, G-code, STEP and IGES files are read on every platform for their own details: the title, designer, printer, filament, the program that exported them, and part names. `carrel-provider-cad` draws STEP and IGES models too; it needs OpenCascade, so for now it's built from source (`docs/guide/getting-started.md`) rather than included in the archives below.
- **Images:** Apple's descriptions lift routing from 63% to 85% when Apple's model files on its own, in the benchmark's new image set; with Gemma 4 E4B as the second opinion, images route right either way.
- **`carrel formats`** lists the file types this device reads, and those it couldn't read so far.

## Ahead of v0.4

These are in this release, but they belong to v0.4, explorer and search, and may change before it:

- **`carrel search QUERY`** finds items by the words in their cards and text, and collection documents and glossary facts, best first, with the passage that matched. Filters narrow it: `collection:`, `tag:`, `kind:`, `after:`, `before:`, `sensitivity:` and `checked-out`.
- **The `embed` provider capability** and each archive's embedding model (EmbeddingGemma 300M by default for new archives), the groundwork for searching by meaning.

## Install

Download the archive for your platform below, unpack it, and put its programs together in a folder on your `PATH`. Each archive includes Kopia and every provider for its platform except `carrel-provider-cad`, with `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and each archive has a build attestation (`gh attestation verify FILE --repo HannahARose/carrel`).

The [user guide](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/User-Guide) explains each part, and [Command-Line Tool](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Command-Line-Tool) lists every command.
