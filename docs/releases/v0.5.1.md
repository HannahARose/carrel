Carrel now has a Mac app. It's a native window over the same archive and the same core as the `carrel` command: review and questions, checking out and returning work, the desk, browsing with previews, search, history with undo, backups and settings, all in one place. It comes with a background agent that keeps noticing your work while the app is closed. The command line and its interactive mode can now do everything the app can, on macOS, Linux and Windows, so you can use whichever suits the moment. Everything still stays on your computer. This is a 0.x release, so anything may change before 1.0.

New here? Start with [Getting started](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Getting-Started), and for the app, [The Mac app](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Mac-App).

## The Mac app

- **One window per archive,** with a sidebar of the interactive mode's screens, a list, and an inspector for the chosen item's card. It needs macOS 26 on Apple silicon.
- **Status:** the archive at a glance, the worker's progress, Recent, and suggestions.
- **Review and Questions:**
  - approve, hold, move or reject proposals, one at a time or a selection;
  - expand or keep compressed files, and keep duplicates or file them as versions;
  - answer and dismiss questions.
- **Corrections and editing:** ⌘K corrects any card in plain words and shows the changes before saving; ⌘E edits its fields in place, checked as the command line checks them.
- **Active:** check items out (with a due date if you like), put folders on the desk, and return, cancel or archive them. Approve, combine, edit or drop the work-session entries.
- **Browse:**
  - the collection tree, as a list or a grid of thumbnails;
  - previews in the inspector, from Quick Look, with Carrel's own drawings for STEP, IGES and 3MF files;
  - New Collection…, Rename… and Expand…, each undoable.
- **Search (⌘F):** the same search as `carrel search`, by meaning and by name, with filters and the matched passages highlighted.
- **Adding items:** drag files and folders onto the window, or File → Add to Archive… (⌘O).
- **History** with undo, explaining what undoing restores and what it doesn't. **Backups:** set up, snapshot, verify and restore. **Settings:** this Mac's and the archive's settings, models, providers and the search index.
- **The app** has its own icon: the carrel, its lamp looking through your collections.

**Getting it:** this release's archives hold the command line, its providers and Kopia, not the app. Signed and notarized builds of the app come with v1.0. Until then, download a development build from CI or build it with Xcode, as [The Mac app](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Mac-App) explains.

## The background agent

- **On a Mac with the app,** a login item notices work sessions as they happen, drafts queued items as they arrive, keeps the search index current and takes scheduled snapshots. A menu bar list shows what's checked out and on the desk, with Pause.
- **Everywhere else, `carrel agent`** does the same from the command line. `carrel agent install` sets it up as a launchd agent on macOS, a systemd user service on Linux, or a task at log-on on Windows, and says exactly what it wrote. It steps aside for the Mac app's agent.

## Every interface fully capable

Anything Carrel can do is now available from the command line, the interactive mode and the app, each in its own way. The interactive mode gained:

- checking out from Browse, and adding to, removing from and archiving the desk;
- creating collections, renaming items and expanding filed compressed files;
- the search index's state, rebuilding it and switching models;
- opening, switching and creating archives;
- the providers in use and the file types this device can't read;
- previews of items in terminals that can draw images (Kitty, WezTerm, Ghostty, iTerm2, and Sixel);
- installing and showing the background agent.

The [Interfaces](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Interfaces) page lists every capability in each interface, and the few deliberate differences.

## Ahead of v0.6

These are in this release, but they belong to v0.6, the librarian and discovery, and may change before it:

- **The librarian:** `carrel ask` answers questions about what's in your archive, with the items and passages it used, and suggests directions as well as answering. In the Mac app it's a panel, with "Ask about this" on any item. By default it only suggests changes; with `librarian.changes` on, it makes the ones it's sure of itself, each undoable.
- **Discovery:** `carrel discover` shows what happened on this day in other years, wanders the stacks one item at a time, and draws a timeline, a map of the archive and your activity. It's also in the Mac app's sidebar.
- **Better questions:** new kinds (about an item, a group, or a sender), ranked by what an answer would settle, with suggested answers and cheap skipping.

## About the version number

This is the first published release with the Mac app. v0.5.0 was tagged but never published: its build stopped while writing the third-party license notices, because they didn't yet accept the license of a new dependency (the agent's file watcher, in the public domain). v0.5.1 is the same release with that fixed, and CI now checks the notices' license list on every change.

## Install

Download the archive for your platform below, unpack it, and put its programs together in a folder on your `PATH`. Each archive includes Kopia and every provider for its platform except `carrel-provider-cad`, with `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and each archive has a build attestation (`gh attestation verify FILE --repo HannahARose/carrel`).

The [user guide](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/User-Guide) explains each part, and [Command-Line Tool](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Command-Line-Tool) lists every command.
