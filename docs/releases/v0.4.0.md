Carrel can now find things the way you remember them. Describe an item in your own words, such as "the lease for the flat on Cowley Road" or "that paper about light-sheet microscopes", and Carrel finds it, whether or not those words are in its title. Search reads what's inside your files, and it runs on your computer with a small embedding model, so nothing leaves it. Browsing shows each item's surroundings too: its history, the items related to it, and what changed lately. This is a 0.x release, so anything may change before 1.0; the Mac app follows in v0.5.

New here? Start with [Getting started](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Getting-Started).

## Search

- **`carrel search QUERY`** finds items by meaning and by their words together, best first, with the passage that matched and why. Exact names and numbers ("invoice 2057-4038", "AS1115") still come first. Filters narrow it: `collection:`, `tag:`, `kind:`, `after:`, `before:`, `sensitivity:` and `checked-out`. `--json` gives the results to other programs.
- **`/` in Browse** runs the same search in the interactive mode, with the matched words highlighted above the card.
- **Searching by meaning** uses the archive's embedding model, EmbeddingGemma 300M by default (334 MB): `carrel models download embeddinggemma-300m`, then `carrel index rebuild`. Each item's card is embedded, and up to 20 passages of its text, so a long document is found by what's inside it. **Restricted items are embedded by their card only,** so nothing in their text can be found, or rebuilt, from the index.
- **The index keeps itself current.** Filing, corrections, edits, returns and undo start the worker, which embeds what changed in the background. Until it catches up, those items are found by their words, and search says so. `carrel index status` shows how far it's got.
- **Switching models:** `carrel index model NAME` builds a new model's index beside the old one, while search keeps using the old one, then switches every device. Qwen3-Embedding 0.6B is the larger alternative.
- **More ways to run the models:** llama.cpp on every platform, MLX on Apple silicon, and Apple's own sentence embedding for archives used only on Apple devices.

## How well it finds things

The benchmark has a new findability test (`carrel bench --search`): 230 descriptions of 81 items, written by hand the way a person would search months later, never by file name. On its first run, EmbeddingGemma found 97% of them in the top five. Search's final merge of words and meaning matches that, and puts exact names and numbers first; words alone found 90%. The results are in [docs/bench](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/blob/main/docs/bench/README.md#findability).

## Browsing

- **Related items:** an item's view in Browse and `carrel show` lists its links (`version-of`, `related`, `part-of`) and the items nearest it in meaning, so a paper leads to its notes and an invoice to its receipt. Enter goes to one.
- **History and Recent,** which arrived in v0.3, show every change that touched an item, with undo, and what was filed, changed or worked on lately.

## From your editor

- **Neovim** (`editors/nvim`, 0.9 or later): `:CarrelSearch` lists results in Telescope when it's installed (with the card in the preview), or in quickfix or a floating window. `:CarrelShow` shows the current file's card in a split, and `:CarrelIndex` the index status.
- **Emacs** (`editors/emacs/carrel.el`, 27.1 or later): `carrel-search` works with vertico, ivy or the default completion, and `carrel-show` shows a card.
- Both drive `carrel` itself, with no server and no other plugins. Installing them is in the user guide.

## Also in this release

- A same-size edit made within one clock tick of Carrel's last look is now noticed on every platform, so a quick save during a work session is never missed.
- The Mac app's design is accepted (`docs/design/mac-app.md`), and the core has the pieces it needs, ahead of v0.5.

## Install

Download the archive for your platform below, unpack it, and put its programs together in a folder on your `PATH`. Each archive includes Kopia and every provider for its platform except `carrel-provider-cad`, with `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and each archive has a build attestation (`gh attestation verify FILE --repo HannahARose/carrel`).

The [user guide](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/User-Guide) explains each part, and [Command-Line Tool](https://github.com/HannahARose/carrel/wiki/Command-Line-Tool) lists every command.
