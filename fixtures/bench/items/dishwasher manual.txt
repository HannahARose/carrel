QuietClean 45 slimline dishwasher: user manual

Before first use
1. Fill the salt container with dishwasher salt.
2. Set the water hardness to your area's level (see table 3).

Programmes
- Eco 50: everyday loads, lowest energy
- Intensive 70: pans and baked-on food
- Quick 35: lightly soiled items

Cleaning the filter: twist anticlockwise and rinse under the tap once a week.
