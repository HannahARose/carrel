# Sourdough loaf

- 500 g strong white flour
- 350 g water
- 100 g active starter
- 10 g salt

Mix, rest 1 hour, add salt. Four sets of folds over 2 hours. Shape, prove in the fridge overnight. Bake at 240 C in a lidded pot, 20 min covered, 25 uncovered.

My note: 330 g water works better with this flour.
