# Receipts

## Purpose and scope

Receipts, invoices and order confirmations for things bought, so they can be returned, claimed under warranty, or claimed as expenses.

## Not here

Instructions for the things bought go in Manuals. Bills for the flat's utilities go in Home. Bank statements go in Finances.
